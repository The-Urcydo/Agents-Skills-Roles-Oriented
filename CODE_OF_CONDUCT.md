# Code of conduct

This repository is worked on by human maintainers and AI agents, and it welcomes
contributions from other people. The same standards apply to everyone who takes
part, whether human or agent.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, and say what you mean
  plainly.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Show evidence. Claims about what works come with the command that was run and
  its result.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project, and keep the reasoning in the open
  so others can follow it.
- Do not ask an agent to bypass the owner's approval, hide what it did, or skip
  a required check.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a point after it has been decided.
- Misrepresenting work: claiming checks passed when they did not, or changing a
  check so that it passes.
- Instructing an agent to exfiltrate data, run destructive commands, or evade
  review.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, comments, and any other space where
someone acts on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

The owners are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct. They may remove
or edit comments, close or lock a discussion, and block someone temporarily or
permanently, depending on how serious and how repeated the behavior is.

To report a problem, contact an owner through the contact details on their
GitHub profile, if listed. The owner accounts are in
[.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS). If you cannot find a private way to
reach them, open a new issue with the **Request a private channel** form,
without describing the incident. An owner will answer with a private way to
share it. Reports are handled privately and treated with care.
